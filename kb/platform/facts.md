# Platform facts (dated; re-check by the date given)

## plat.instagram: Instagram
for: brain, copy, art | edit: owner

Verified Nov 2026, re-check by Feb 2027.
- Carousels: the app allows 20 slides, the publishing API allows 10. Our hard max is 10.
- Publish at 4:5 (1080x1350). The API rejects 3:4, and every slide is cropped to the first slide's ratio. The profile grid shows a 3:4 crop, so keep text and faces in the centre.
- Carousels that are not swiped are often re-shown from slide 2 (Mosseri, Oct 2024).
- Top ranking signals: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach; sends matter most for reaching non-followers (Mosseri, Jan 2025). "Sends weigh 3 to 5x likes" is NOT an Instagram statement.
- 2026 direction: originality and raw, real-looking content are favoured over polished AI-looking content.
- Benchmarks: carousels lead on engagement and saves (Metricool 2026: about 9x the saves of single images); Reels reach more non-followers. If follower growth stalls after 60 to 90 days, add music to carousels and test Reels.
- The first ~125 caption characters show before "more"; captions are indexed for search.
- Most views and interactions arrive within 72 hours.
