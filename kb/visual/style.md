# Brand visual style

## style.look: Brand look
for: art | edit: owner

Bright and warm editorial wellness: a premium food and lifestyle magazine, never a pharmacy or stock photo. Model-quality people in real places, real products, genuinely happy faces. Confident, fresh, a little playful. Lots of clean space for text. Real photography feel over glossy AI renders (Instagram ranks originality; the "too slick" AI look underperforms).

## style.palette: Palette
for: art, image | edit: owner

Text and accents: forest green #0F3B24, leaf green #07803B, warm cream #F6F1E7, white, near-black; coral #D0473E as a highlight. Product accents: strawberry red (gummies), honey gold (honey sticks), sage (capsules), rust orange (TRUBLK). Text must contrast with what is behind it: light area -> forest green or near-black; dark area or saturated colour field -> cream or white. Coral and the product accents are for a marker stroke or highlight band only, never for lines of text on a background of the same colour family.

## style.light: Light and surfaces
for: art | edit: owner

Bright natural daylight or hard morning sun with crisp clean shadows; warm and airy. Surfaces: linen, raw wood, travertine, ceramic, glass, cotton net bags, fresh produce, real outdoor and home locations.
Avoid: dark moody scenes, neon, purple or cold blue casts, clutter, cheesy smiles, anything clinical, plain coloured paper backdrops (except the pop look colour field), generic gradient templates.

## style.hero: Every slide needs a hero subject
for: art | edit: learn

A person, our product, or a real object (ingredient, cup, plant, notebook, phone page) that fills the frame. Never an empty backdrop or a plain colour field on its own; never empty still lifes of random props, books or old pages.
