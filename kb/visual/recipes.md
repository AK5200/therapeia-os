# Visual recipes

The Brain picks one visual_recipe as a scene hint; the Art Director loads only that section and applies it inside the chosen look.

## recipe.list: Recipe list (for the Brain)
for: brain | edit: learn

botanical_macro, calm_lifestyle_scene, comparison_split, editorial_ingredient_flatlay, product_hero_scene, routine_scene, science_editorial, typographic_slide. product_hero_scene only for conversion and retention.

## recipe.botanical_macro: Botanical macro
for: art | edit: learn

COLOUR FIELD macro. Extreme close-up of one natural texture filling the frame (ashwagandha root fibres, a ripe strawberry, honeycomb cells, raw dark mountain rock), sharp detail, against a clean backdrop in the post's colour. Hard light that shows the texture. Leave one calm side for text.

## recipe.calm_lifestyle_scene: Calm lifestyle scene
for: art | edit: learn

EDITORIAL MODEL or SUN-DRENCHED STILL LIFE. A real-looking adult in a quiet everyday moment (by a sunny window, on a balcony, in a linen-filled bedroom), cropped per the model rules, or the same moment told through objects only: a ceramic mug, a linen napkin, a plant, a book face down. Warm natural light, bright and airy, calm but not sleepy.

## recipe.comparison_split: Comparison split
for: art | edit: learn

CREAM STUDIO split. Two clearly different subjects side by side on one warm cream seamless backdrop, same light and lens on both, e.g. a small heap of raw dried ashwagandha root next to a neat ceramic dish of fine powder. Clean gap down the middle, both subjects in the lower half, top half empty for text.

## recipe.editorial_ingredient_flatlay: Editorial ingredient flatlay
for: art | edit: learn

CREAM STUDIO. Raw ingredients styled as hero objects on warm cream or oat seamless paper: dried ashwagandha roots, a few orange ashwagandha berries, sage leaves, a ceramic spoon of powder. Either one tidy cluster bleeding off the bottom edge with the top 40 percent empty, or a sparse top-down flatlay with lots of air. Soft top light, gentle shadows.

## recipe.product_hero_scene: Product hero scene
for: art | edit: learn

INGREDIENT BED or COLOUR FIELD. The closed product, front-facing and sharp, nested at a slight angle in an overflowing bed of its signature ingredient (strawberries for the gummies, honeycomb and vanilla flowers for the honey sticks, dried roots and sage for the capsules, dark mountain rock and copper for TRUBLK), or on a real surface (travertine, dark stone, raw linen) with dramatic directional light, never a plain colour backdrop. Top-down or 45 degrees, hard morning sun, crisp shadows, one or two loose ingredients for depth.

## recipe.routine_scene: Routine scene
for: art | edit: learn

SUN-DRENCHED STILL LIFE. The objects of one routine moment on a kitchen counter or nightstand in hard window light with long shadows: a glass of water, a ceramic mug, the closed product (if allowed), fresh fruit in a cotton net bag, a folded linen napkin. Implied, never staged; clear order; bright.

## recipe.science_editorial: Science editorial
for: art | edit: learn

CLEAN EDITORIAL SCIENCE on cream. Clear glassware, a ceramic mortar with powder, a pipette resting on a glass dish, dried roots, arranged like a still life on travertine or cream paper in soft daylight. Precise and calm, bright, never clinical: no lab coats, charts, data screens or hospital feel.

## recipe.typographic_slide: Typographic slide
for: art | edit: learn

CREAM STUDIO text slide. Warm cream seamless paper with a very faint paper grain, almost empty, with one small hero object (a single strawberry, a sprig of sage, a root, a ceramic cup) sitting in the bottom corner or bleeding off the bottom edge. Top 60 percent completely calm for big text.
