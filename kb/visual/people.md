# People in images (single master copy)

## people.audience: Who appears
for: art, image, design_critic | edit: locked

Only white or Black people, sometimes East Asian: roughly 45% white, 40% Black, 15% East Asian. Never South Asian, Indian, Latino or Middle Eastern. Rotate ethnicity and gender against recent posts.
Ages 22 to 40, clearly adult. Never children or anyone who could look under 21. Never pregnant people.

## people.framing: Crop, pose and expression
for: art, image, design_critic | edit: locked

- Crop by risk: hand only < face hidden or turned < chin down < full face. The cover of a carousel that shows a person, and every single image with a person, uses a FULL FACE (never cut at the nose or chin). At most 2 full faces per carousel; most inside slides show no face.
- Expression: happy (soft closed-mouth smile reaching the eyes), confident or serene. Never laughing or an open-mouth grin.
- Tasteful framing: no swimwear, midriff or body-focused shots.
- The product is held in one hand, label to camera, never at or near the lips.
- A model is never presented as a customer, and is never shown with a result on their body.

## people.message: The picture supports the words
for: art, design_critic | edit: locked

Never show what the copy denies. If the copy says "not sleepy", show someone alert, bright-eyed and active, never eyes closed or resting. Calm = present and awake.
If person is null the slide shows no people at all; if only a hand appears, use crop hand_only.
