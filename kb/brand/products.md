## product.rules: How products may appear
for: art, image, design_critic | edit: locked

- Every post shows a real product: the cover (held by a person or as a product hero) and the last slide. If the brief has no product, feature one in the visuals only, rotating daily, never as an ad.
- Only products we have reference photos for. Sealed, front label to camera, lid or cap on top, true-to-life size.
- Never redraw, simplify, translate or invent label text or artwork. Never open packs or show views we have no photo of.
- Never at or near the lips. Never several products lined up.
