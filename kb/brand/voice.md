# Brand voice

## voice.who: Who we are
for: brain, copy, critic, art | edit: locked

Therapeia Zoi is a premium US wellness brand selling KSM-66 ashwagandha and TRUBLK shilajit supplements (gummies, capsules, honey sticks, resin sachets). Our message: feel good inside and out. Audience: US adults 25 to 45 dealing with everyday stress, sleep, energy and focus. Tagline idea: "Calm by night. Drive by day."

## voice.tone: Tone
for: copy, critic | edit: owner

Warm, confident and vivid; never hype, fear or shame. Science-literate. A knowledgeable friend, not a salesperson. Short sentences, plain words. Write like people talk: real questions, an honest short answer first ("Short answer: no.", "Honest answer: it's earthy."), light warmth or humour. Educate, never attack, shame or scare.

## voice.words: Words we avoid
for: copy, critic | edit: owner

Never: miracle, life-changing, game-changer, hack, revolutionary, superfood, magic. (Claim words such as cure, treat, heal, detox, guaranteed, clinically proven live in compliance/rules.md.)
Max 2 emojis per caption, none on slides. Always spell the hashtag #TherapeiaZoi exactly like that.

## voice.punctuation: Punctuation
for: copy, critic | edit: owner

Never use em dashes or en dashes in slides, captions or hashtags. Use a full stop, comma or colon, or split into two sentences. Avoid hyphenated words unless part of a name or fixed term (KSM-66, third-party tested).

## voice.facts: Facts and numbers
for: brain, copy, critic | edit: locked

Follow comp.principles rules 1 and 2: facts and numbers come only from the approved facts and product label notes supplied with the task. A fact that appears only in a brief's key points or a competitor idea is NOT approved. If a point needs a fact we do not have, write it without the fact or drop the point.
