# Carousel anatomy

Every carousel has one PROMISE and one GOAL SIGNAL (save, send, comment or follow). Every slide has exactly one job. The slides carry the substance; the caption is a bonus (viral.reader).

## anat.principles: Core principles
for: brain, copy, critic | edit: learn

- One promise per post, delivered fully on the slides. Clickbait the body does not pay off kills trust.
- Follow the story in viral.arc; one idea per slide; if a slide adds nothing new, cut it.
- Every slide earns the next swipe: it answers the last slide and ends on a pull.
- Plain words a 12-year-old understands; explain any science word in the same sentence.
- Slides must make full sense without the caption. Structure is borrowed from winners; wording and design are always our own.

## anat.cover: Slide 1, the cover
for: copy, critic, art | edit: learn

Job: stop the scroll for ONE specific person (viral.reader).
- Headline max 10 words: the reader's moment, a surprising true fact, a myth, or permission. Specific beats clever; readable at thumbnail size.
- Uses the chosen hook (carousel/hooks.md).
- Body: one open-loop line, max 10 words ("Here's what's actually going on...", "It's not your mattress...").
- Critical text and any face sit in the centre so the 3:4 profile grid crop does not cut them.

## anat.rehook: Slide 2, the re-hook (second cover)
for: copy, critic, art | edit: learn

Instagram often re-shows a skipped carousel starting from slide 2. So slide 2 must work as a cover ON ITS OWN.
- Name and validate the moment ("You're not imagining it."), or state the most surprising true point, then pull to slide 3.
- Never a warm-up or a bare "Tip 1".

## anat.body: Body slides
for: copy, critic, art | edit: learn

Job: teach one thing per slide, so well the reader could explain it to a friend.
- Headline max 8 words, then a body of 15 to 35 words in 2 or 3 short sentences.
- Mechanism slides: what happens in the body, in plain words, with one concrete detail (a time of day, a body signal, a simple comparison).
- Action slides: one specific step (when, how long, what to swap), never "be mindful" or "listen to your body".
- The last sentence pulls to the next slide. Numbered progress for lists and steps.

## anat.payoff: The payoff slide (second to last)
for: copy, critic, art | edit: learn

The screenshot slide: a short checklist (3 to 5 items, a few words each) or the one-line takeaway. It must stand alone as a screenshot and is the slide most likely to be sent. Every carousel declares it as `send_slide`.

## anat.cta: The last slide, one CTA
for: copy, critic, art | edit: learn

Ask for ONE thing, matched to goal_signal and allowed by the funnel stage:
- send (default for awareness): "Send this to the friend who [specific moment]"
- save: "Save this for [specific moment, e.g. tonight at 10pm]"
- comment: "Which one are you?" or "Comment [WORD]" (only if we really reply)
- follow: "Follow for one calm, science-backed tip a week"
- product CTA ("link in bio") only in conversion and retention. On product posts the last slide shows the product clearly.

## anat.count: Slide count and limits
for: brain, copy, critic | edit: learn

Default 8 slides (7 to 10); short carousels of 2 to 4 slides underperform, so never go under 5 (5 or 6 only for myth_fact with 2 myths or tweet_style). Hard max 10. Never pad.
Text limits: cover headline 6 to 10 words + body max 10; other headlines max 8 words; body max 35 words in max 3 short sentences; payoff checklist max 5 items. No hashtags, emojis or links on slides.

## anat.caption: Caption pairing
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

The caption is a bonus, never where the main content lives. Instagram: line 1 (max 125 characters) restates the promise in new words with a searchable phrase ("ashwagandha and sleep"); then 2 to 5 short lines that add something (one extra tip, why it matters, sources); the CTA; 3 to 5 hashtags including #TherapeiaZoi. Research details are added by the system as the STUDY NOTE.
TikTok: max 300 characters, words people search, 3 to 5 hashtags.
alt_text: one plain sentence describing the cover image.

## anat.single: Single image posts
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

Single images reach the fewest people, so one only earns its place as a line worth sending: a validation statement, a permission line, or a tiny routine. Headline 6 to 10 words; body optional, max 25 words, one specific useful thing. Never a topic announcement.
