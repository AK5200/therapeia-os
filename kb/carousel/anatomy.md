# Carousel anatomy

Every carousel has one PROMISE and one GOAL SIGNAL (save, send, comment or follow). Every slide has exactly one job. Slide text is drawn by an image model, so words stay short; the caption carries the detail.

## anat.principles: Core principles
for: brain, copy, critic | edit: learn

- One promise per post, delivered fully. Clickbait the body does not pay off kills trust.
- One idea per slide. If a slide adds nothing new, cut it.
- Every slide must earn the next swipe: progress, a small open loop every 2 or 3 slides ("the 4th one surprises people"), or a numbered step ("3/6").
- Slides must make sense without the caption.
- Structure is borrowed from winners; wording and design are always our own.

## anat.cover: Slide 1, the cover
for: copy, critic, art | edit: learn

Job: stop the scroll and promise a specific payoff.
- Headline max 8 words, one idea, specific beats clever. Readable at thumbnail size (2 cm wide).
- Uses the chosen hook formula (carousel/hooks.md).
- Body = a short swipe cue ("swipe, we'll keep it short ->", "the honest answer ->").
- Critical text and any face sit in the centre so the 3:4 profile grid crop does not cut them.

## anat.rehook: Slide 2, the re-hook (second cover)
for: copy, critic, art | edit: learn

Instagram often re-shows a skipped carousel starting from slide 2 (Mosseri, 2024). So slide 2 must work as a cover ON ITS OWN, for someone who never saw slide 1.
- Restate the stakes from a new angle: a surprising true fact from the brief, "why this matters", "most people get this wrong", or the first and best item.
- Never a bare continuation like "Tip 1: drink water" or a warm-up slide.
- Strong headline, readable alone.

## anat.body: Body slides
for: copy, critic, art | edit: learn

Job: deliver the promise, one idea each.
- Headline max 7 words, body max 18 words in max 3 short lines.
- Pattern: headline, then 1 or 2 supporting lines.
- Use numbered progress when the archetype is a list or steps.

## anat.payoff: The payoff slide (second to last)
for: copy, critic, art | edit: learn

The screenshot-worthy summary, checklist or "the answer". It must stand alone as a screenshot and is the slide most likely to be sent. Put the highest-value content here, not on the CTA slide. Every carousel declares which slide is the `send_slide`.

## anat.cta: The last slide, one CTA
for: copy, critic, art | edit: learn

Ask for ONE thing, matched to goal_signal and allowed by the funnel stage:
- save: "Save this for your next [moment]"
- send: "Send this to the friend who [relatable trait]"
- comment: "Comment [WORD] and [what happens]" (only if we really reply) or "Which one are you?"
- follow: "Follow for one calm, science-backed tip a week"
- product CTA ("link in bio", "Shop Calm") only in conversion and retention.
On product posts the last slide shows the product clearly.

## anat.count: Slide count and limits
for: brain, copy, critic | edit: learn

Default 7 slides (6 to 9). Quick formats 5 or 6. Deep dives 8 or 9. Hard max 10 (publishing API limit). Never pad.
Text: cover headline max 8 words; other headlines max 7; body max 18 words, max 3 lines; never more than 4 lines of text on a slide. No hashtags, emojis or links on slides.

## anat.caption: Caption pairing
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

Instagram: line 1 (max 125 characters, all people see before "more") restates the promise in different words from the cover and includes a searchable phrase ("ashwagandha and sleep"). Then 3 to 6 short lines that ADD context (never repeat the slides), sources for any study, the CTA, and 3 to 5 relevant hashtags including #TherapeiaZoi.
TikTok: max 300 characters, written with words people search, 3 to 5 hashtags.
alt_text: one plain sentence describing the cover image.

## anat.single: Single image posts
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

One slide. Headline max 6 words: one specific line that stops the scroll (a relatable moment, a question, or a short routine name like "my 9pm reset"). Body optional, max 12 words. The caption carries the detail.
