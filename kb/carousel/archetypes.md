# Archetypes

The Brain picks one archetype per post; Copy and the Critic load only that section. Slide maps assume the default count; shrink or grow the BODY, never the cover, re-hook, payoff or CTA. Signals: save, send, comment, follow (practitioner consensus, to be confirmed by our own data in learnings/performance.md).

## arch.myth_fact: Myth vs fact
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

1 cover: the myth in the reader's own words, or "You've been told X. Here's what research says."
2 re-hook: why this myth matters ("This myth is why your evenings feel rushed").
3 to N-2: one myth per slide, headline "Myth: ...", body "Fact: ..." with one plain reason. Max 3 myths.
N-1 payoff: myth vs fact summary on one slide.
N CTA: send ("Send this to the person who still believes #2").
Tone: friendly, never mocking.

## arch.listicle: Numbered list
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

1 cover: number + specific outcome ("5 evening habits for a calmer night").
2 re-hook: the best item first, framed as a hook.
3 to N-2: one item per slide, numbered, each with a one-line why.
N-1 payoff: checklist recap of every item.
N CTA: save.

## arch.how_to: Step by step
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

1 cover: outcome + time cost ("A 10 minute wind-down that works").
2 re-hook: "Most people skip step 3" or why the routine matters.
3 to N-2: one numbered step per slide, starting with a verb.
N-1 payoff: the whole routine on one slide.
N CTA: save (or product routine CTA in conversion/retention).

## arch.comparison: This or that
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

1 cover: "X or Y: which fits you?" (root powder vs standardized extract, gummies vs capsules, morning vs evening, resin vs capsules).
2 re-hook: the one criterion that decides it.
3 to N-2: one criterion per slide, fair to both sides.
N-1 payoff: "Pick X if..., pick Y if...".
N CTA: comment ("Which are you?").
Never attack another brand. Never name competitors.

## arch.what_happens: What happens when / timeline
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

1 cover: timeline promise ("What 8 weeks of a calm evening routine looks like").
2 re-hook: "Week 1 is where most people quit".
3 to N-2: one stage per slide. Only structure/function outcomes that are in the brief; routine milestones are always safe.
N-1 payoff: who it suits and the honest caveats.
N CTA: send or save.

## arch.ingredient_deep_dive: Ingredient explained
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

1 cover: "The ingredient behind [benefit], explained in 7 slides".
2 re-hook: origin or one surprising true fact.
3 to N-2: what it is, where it comes from, how it is studied (only brief facts), quality markers, what to look for on a label.
N-1 payoff: one-slide summary.
N CTA: follow.

## arch.study_breakdown: A study, simply explained
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

Only when the brief contains a research fact. Open with the reader's problem, not the paper.
1 cover: the reader's moment or the question the study asked ("Researchers asked: can stressed adults feel calmer in 8 weeks?").
2 re-hook: why this question matters to the reader, in plain words.
3 the setup in plain words (who, how long, compared with what), 4 the finding as its approved slide wording, 5 what it does NOT mean (one study, one extract, results vary).
N-1 payoff: what the reader can take from it today.
N CTA: save or send. The full study is in the caption note.

## arch.story: Ritual story (a moment in an ordinary day)
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

A short scene from an ordinary day told in second person ("You get home at 7..."), built around a routine: tea, a walk, putting the phone away, taking the gummies with dinner.
1 cover: the moment in one line. 2 re-hook: why this moment matters. 3 to N-2: the ritual, one step per slide. N-1 payoff: the line worth saving. N CTA: follow or comment.
NEVER a founder story, a customer story, a named or first-person character with a backstory, or anything presented as something that really happened to a real person. No results, feelings after taking the product, or before/after.

## arch.routine_shift: Old habit vs new habit
for: copy, critic | edit: locked

Swaps HABITS, never outcomes. "Swap scrolling in bed for 10 minutes with a book."
1 cover: the swap in one line. 2 re-hook: why small swaps stick. 3 to N-2: one swap per slide, headline "Swap X for Y", body one plain reason. N-1 payoff: the full list of swaps. N CTA: save.
NEVER write the words "before", "after", "before and after" or "then vs now". Never describe how someone feels, sleeps or performs after the swap (no "you sleep through", "calmer mornings"). Never depict anyone unwell. Never imply the product caused anything.

## arch.checklist: What to look for
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

1 cover: "Save this before you buy [ashwagandha]".
2 re-hook: why it matters.
3 to N-2: one checklist item per slide (standardized extract, third-party testing, dose shown on the label, clear form).
N-1 payoff: the full checklist on one slide.
N CTA: save.

## arch.mistakes: Mistakes to avoid
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

1 cover: "5 mistakes that waste your [supplement] routine".
2 re-hook: the most common mistake.
3 to N-2: mistake, why, fix (one per slide).
N-1 payoff: recap.
N CTA: save or send.
Never shame the reader.

## arch.faq: Honest answers
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

1 cover: the real question ("Wait, what is shilajit?").
2 to N-1: one real question per slide as the headline, body opens with a short confident answer ("Short answer: no.") and one plain line.
N CTA: comment ("Ask yours below").
Questions ONLY about the product and routine: what it is, what it tastes like, how to take it, gummies vs capsules, when to take it, how long a bottle lasts, how it is tested, where the ingredient comes from. NEVER about safety, side effects, medications, pregnancy, conditions, who should not take it or "is it safe". Leave those topics out entirely.

## arch.quiz: Pick your type
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

1 cover: "Which evening type are you? Swipe and pick".
2 to N-3: A/B choice prompts.
N-2 to N-1: the result types (lifestyle framing, never a diagnosis).
N CTA: comment ("Comment your letter").

## arch.tweet_style: Short text posts
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

5 or 6 slides, each one short punchy observation in a tweet-card or notes design (pairs with art look 3 notes). Human-sounding, no hype.
Last slide: send CTA.

## arch.relatable: POV / meme moment
for: copy, critic | edit: learn

1 cover: a specific relatable moment ("POV: it's 3pm and your focus left the chat"). 2 to N-2: gentle escalation. N-1: the pivot to one useful tip. N CTA: send or follow.
Real and a little funny, never dramatic. Use sparingly.
