# Structure rubric

## rubric.score: 20-point pre-publish score
for: critic, watcher | edit: learn

Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2, strictly: 2 only when fully met with no doubt, judged as the reader in viral.reader. A typical first draft scores 12 to 16; 20 is rare. For single images score rehook and pacing as 2. Publish at 15 or more AND no zero on hook, rehook, save_send or compliance. Below that: rewrite (max 2 rounds), then flag for a human.

| key | criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hook | STOP | a topic or brand line | clear, but not about the reader | the reader's own moment, a surprising fact, a myth or permission; would stop a fast thumb |
| rehook | Slide 2 alone | continuation | partly standalone | works as a cover, validates or surprises |
| one_idea | One idea per slide | several | mostly one | strictly one |
| pacing | SWIPE | slides could be shuffled | logical but flat | every slide answers the last and pulls to the next |
| density | Substance in plain words | vague lines or "see caption" slides | some slides teach | every slide teaches one specific thing a 12-year-old understands |
| payoff | Payoff slide | promise not delivered | delivered, forgettable | screenshot-worthy checklist or takeaway |
| cta | CTA | none or several | generic | one specific ask (send to whom, save for when) |
| design | Legibility + consistency | low contrast, mixed styles | mostly consistent | thumbnail-legible, grid-safe, one system |
| save_send | SEND / SAVE | no reason | one of the two | someone would send it to a named kind of friend AND save it |
| compliance | Compliance + honesty | disease claim, invented fact | borderline wording | structure/function only, facts only from the brief |

The copy critic scores everything except `design` (scored later by the design critic); use 2 as a placeholder for design at copy stage.

## rubric.watcher: Extra fields for competitor breakdowns
for: watcher | edit: learn

For every analysed competitor carousel also record: archetype (id from arch.list or "other"), hook_type (id from hook.list or "other"), slide_roles (cover, rehook, body, payoff, cta per slide), send_slide number, and one line on what made it saveable or sendable. The Brain adapts these structures, never the wording.
