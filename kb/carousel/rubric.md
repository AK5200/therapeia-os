# Structure rubric

## rubric.score: 20-point pre-publish score
for: critic, watcher | edit: learn

Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2. Publish at 15 or more AND no zero on hook, rehook, save_send or compliance. Below that: rewrite (max 2 rounds), then flag for a human.

| key | criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hook | Hook strength | vague or generic | clear topic, weak tension | specific promise + tension, max 8 words |
| rehook | Slide 2 re-hook | pure continuation | partly standalone | works as a cover on its own |
| one_idea | One idea per slide | several | mostly one | strictly one |
| pacing | Pacing | random or filler | logical but flat | escalates, numbered progress, open loops |
| density | Text density | over limits | at the limit | well under limits, scannable in 3 seconds |
| payoff | Payoff slide | promise not delivered | delivered, forgettable | screenshot-worthy summary |
| cta | CTA | none or several | generic | one ask matched to goal_signal and funnel |
| design | Legibility + consistency | low contrast, mixed styles | mostly consistent | thumbnail-legible, grid-safe, one system |
| save_send | Save / send value | no reason to keep or send | one of the two | clear save reason AND a sendable slide |
| compliance | Compliance + honesty | disease claim, fake proof | borderline wording | structure/function only, no invented facts or people |

The copy critic scores everything except `design` (scored later by the design critic); use 2 as a placeholder for design at copy stage.

## rubric.watcher: Extra fields for competitor breakdowns
for: watcher | edit: learn

For every analysed competitor carousel also record: archetype (id from arch.list or "other"), hook_type (id from hook.list or "other"), slide_roles (cover, rehook, body, payoff, cta per slide), send_slide number, and one line on what made it saveable or sendable. The Brain adapts these structures, never the wording.
